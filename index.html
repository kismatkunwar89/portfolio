/ Publications

An LLM-Driven Iterative Workflow for Ontological Mapping of Digital Forensic Artifacts

First author and presenter - ICDF2C 2025 - Springer - Published Jul 17, 2026

Evaluated an LLM- and SHACL-based validation workflow for generating JSON-LD representations compliant with the Cyber-investigation Analysis Standard Expression (CASE) and Unified Cyber Ontology (UCO). The study covered Windows Prefetch, LNK, and Event Log artifacts and reached semantic compliance within one or two validation iterations when supplied with explicit ontology constraints.

View publication

An Indicator of Inconsistency Framework for Detecting Contradictory Digital Artifacts

Co-author - Forensic Science International: Digital Investigation, Volume 58 - Elsevier - Sep 2026

Introduced the Indicator of Inconsistency (IoI) Framework, which uses reusable SPARQL rules over knowledge graphs based on the Cyber-investigation Analysis Standard Expression (CASE) and Unified Cyber Ontology (UCO). The framework detects temporal, structural, and semantic contradictions across related forensic artifacts and was evaluated using five anti-forensic scenarios and independent forensic images to characterize false-positive behavior. My contributions included software development, data curation, validation, visualization, and writing.
